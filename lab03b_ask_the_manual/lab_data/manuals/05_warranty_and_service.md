# Quillbrew QB-500 — Limited Warranty and Service

Document QB500-M05 · Revision C

## 1. Warranty Period

Quillbrew warrants the QB-500 against defects in materials and workmanship for 24 months from the date of purchase. Customers who register the product at quillbrew.example/register within 30 days of purchase receive an extended warranty of 36 months in total.

## 2. What Is Covered

The warranty covers repair or replacement of parts that fail under normal household use, including the pump, thermoblock, grinder motor, control board, and brew group. Shipping costs to and from an authorized service center are covered for the first 12 months.

## 3. What Is Not Covered

The warranty does not cover:

- Damage caused by scale buildup when descaling has not been performed as prompted by the CALC indicator.
- Damage caused by descaling products other than Quillbrew DescaleCare.
- Wear parts, including seals, the AquaPure filter, and the burr set after 15,000 brewing cycles.
- Commercial use, such as use in an office with more than 10 users or in a café.
- Damage caused by foreign objects in the grinder.

## 4. Requesting Service

Before requesting service, follow the steps in the Troubleshooting manual. To request service, contact Quillbrew Service with the serial number (found under the drip tray) and proof of purchase. A service agent will issue a return authorization (RMA) number. Machines must be emptied, descaled if possible, and packed in the original packaging or equivalent protection.

## 5. Out-of-Warranty Repairs

Repairs outside the warranty period are available from authorized service centers. A quote is provided after inspection.
