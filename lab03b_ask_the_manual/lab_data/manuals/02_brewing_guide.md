# Quillbrew QB-500 — Brewing Guide

Document QB500-M02 · Revision C

## 1. Beverage Programs

The QB-500 offers six beverage programs: Espresso (40 ml), Doppio (80 ml), Lungo (110 ml), Americano (40 ml espresso + 120 ml hot water), Cappuccino, and Latte Macchiato. Volumes can be adjusted in Menu > Beverages from 20 ml to 240 ml.

## 2. Coffee Strength (Dose)

The dose setting controls how much ground coffee the brew group receives per shot, from 7 g (mild) to 12 g (extra strong). Increasing the dose without adjusting the grind setting can slow the flow rate and increase extraction.

## 3. Extraction Temperature

The thermoblock heats the brew water to one of three extraction temperatures, set in Menu > Settings > Temperature: Low (90 °C), Medium (93 °C, factory default), and High (96 °C). Dark roasts generally extract best at Low; light roasts at High.

## 4. Pre-Infusion

When pre-infusion is enabled, the brew group wets the coffee puck at low pressure for 3 seconds before full 15-bar extraction begins. Pre-infusion reduces channeling, which occurs when water finds a path of least resistance through the puck and leaves parts of the coffee under-extracted.

## 5. Extraction Time

A correctly calibrated espresso takes 25 to 30 seconds from the start of pump pressure to the end of the shot. A shot faster than 20 seconds usually indicates under-extraction; a shot slower than 35 seconds usually indicates over-extraction. The display shows the extraction time after each shot when Menu > Settings > Shot Timer is enabled.

## 6. Milk Texturing with the Steam Wand

Fill a stainless steel pitcher with cold milk to just below the spout. Purge the steam wand for 2 seconds to clear condensed water before texturing. Insert the wand tip just below the milk surface to incorporate air (stretching), then lower the pitcher so the tip is deeper to create a whirlpool (texturing). Stop at 60–65 °C. After texturing, wipe the wand with a damp cloth and purge it again for 2 seconds to clear milk residue from the steam tip holes.
