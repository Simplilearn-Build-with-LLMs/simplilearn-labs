# Quillbrew QB-500 — Troubleshooting

Document QB500-M04 · Revision C

## 1. Error Codes

| Code | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| E01 | Water reservoir not detected | Reseat the reservoir until it clicks. Check that the float magnet is clean. |
| E03 | Brew group not inserted or not locked | Reinsert the brew group and press until it locks. Close the service door. |
| E05 | Grinder blocked | Switch off and unplug the machine. Empty the bean hopper and remove any foreign object from the burr chamber with the cleaning brush. Restart. |
| E07 | Thermoblock temperature sensor fault | Switch off for 30 minutes and restart. If E07 returns, contact Quillbrew Service. |

## 2. Over-Extraction

Symptoms: the espresso tastes bitter, burnt, or harsh, the crema is dark brown with a white spot in the center, and the shot takes longer than 35 seconds. Cause: the water is in contact with the coffee too long or at too high a temperature. Solution: set the grinder 1–2 steps coarser, lower the extraction temperature to Low, or reduce the dose.

## 3. Under-Extraction

Symptoms: the espresso tastes sour, thin, or watery, the crema is pale and disappears quickly, and the shot runs faster than 20 seconds. Cause: the water passes through the coffee too quickly. Solution: set the grinder 1–2 steps finer, increase the dose, or enable pre-infusion to reduce channeling.

## 4. Low Pump Pressure

Symptoms: coffee drips slowly or not at all and the pump sounds louder than usual. Possible causes: air in the hydraulic circuit after the reservoir ran dry, a grind setting that is too fine, or scale buildup. Solution: run the priming procedure, set the grinder coarser, and run a descaling cycle if the CALC indicator is lit.

## 5. Weak Steam or Sputtering Steam Wand

Symptoms: the steam wand sputters, produces wet steam, or cannot create microfoam. Possible causes: condensed water in the wand or clogged steam tip holes. Solution: purge the steam wand for 2 seconds before texturing, and perform the steam wand deep clean described in the Cleaning and Maintenance manual.

## 6. Indicator Lights

A flashing orange ring around the power button means a descaling cycle is due. A steady red ring means the drip tray or grounds container must be emptied. A flashing red ring together with an error code indicates a fault listed in Section 1.
