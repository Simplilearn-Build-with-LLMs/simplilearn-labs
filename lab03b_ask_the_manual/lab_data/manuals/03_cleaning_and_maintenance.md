# Quillbrew QB-500 — Cleaning and Maintenance

Document QB500-M03 · Revision C

## 1. Maintenance Schedule

| Task | Interval |
|---|---|
| Empty drip tray and grounds container | When the float indicator rises or the display shows EMPTY |
| Purge and wipe steam wand | After each milk beverage |
| Rinse brew group | Weekly |
| Lubricate brew group | Every 500 brewing cycles |
| Replace AquaPure filter | Every 2 months or 50 L, whichever comes first |
| Descaling cycle | When the CALC indicator lights up |

## 2. Descaling Cycle

Calcium and magnesium in water form limescale (scale) in the thermoblock and hydraulic circuit. The CALC indicator lights up and the descale ring around the power button flashes orange when descaling is due. With hardness Level 2 and no filter, this occurs approximately every 200 brewing cycles; at Level 4 it occurs approximately every 80 brewing cycles.

To descale, remove the AquaPure filter, pour one 250 ml bottle of Quillbrew DescaleCare solution into the reservoir, fill with water to the CALC line, and start Menu > Maintenance > Descale. The cycle takes about 25 minutes and includes two rinse phases. Do not interrupt the cycle. Use only Quillbrew DescaleCare; acetic acid (vinegar) based products can damage the seals in the hydraulic circuit.

## 3. Brew Group Rinse and Lubrication

Switch off the machine, open the service door on the right side, press the red PUSH button, and slide the brew group out. Rinse it under lukewarm running water without detergent and let it air dry before reinserting. Every 500 brewing cycles, apply a thin layer of Quillbrew food-safe grease to the guide rails. Do not wash the brew group in a dishwasher.

## 4. Steam Wand Deep Clean

If the steam tip holes are clogged with dried milk protein, unscrew the steam tip, soak it in warm water for 10 minutes, and clear each of the three holes with the cleaning pin on the grind adjustment key.

## 5. Bean Hopper

Wipe the bean hopper with a dry cloth monthly to remove coffee oils. Do not use oily, caramelized, or flavored beans, which can clog the conical burrs.
