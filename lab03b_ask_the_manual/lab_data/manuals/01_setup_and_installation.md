# Quillbrew QB-500 Bean-to-Cup Espresso Machine — Setup and Installation

Document QB500-M01 · Revision C

## 1. Package Contents

The QB-500 ships with the following components: the main unit, a removable 1.8 L water reservoir, a 250 g bean hopper with aroma lid, a removable brew group, a drip tray with float indicator, a used-grounds container, an AquaPure water filter cartridge, one water hardness test strip, a grind adjustment key, and a cleaning brush.

## 2. Electrical Requirements

The North American model is rated at 120 V, 60 Hz, 1450 W. Connect the unit directly to a grounded outlet. Do not use an extension cord or a power strip. Leave at least 10 cm of clearance on the left, right, and back of the unit for ventilation.

## 3. Determining Water Hardness

Before first use, dip the supplied test strip in tap water for 1 second and wait 1 minute. Count the number of red squares on the strip and set the water hardness level in Menu > Settings > Water Hardness:

| Red squares | Hardness level | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1 | Level 1 | Soft |
| 2 | Level 2 | Medium |
| 3 | Level 3 | Hard |
| 4 | Level 4 | Very hard |

The hardness level determines how often the machine prompts for a descaling cycle. An incorrect setting can lead to scale buildup in the hydraulic circuit.

## 4. Installing the Water Filter

Soak the AquaPure cartridge in cold water for 5 minutes, then screw it into the filter socket at the bottom of the reservoir. Activate it in Menu > Settings > Water Filter > Install. When a filter is installed and activated, the machine automatically extends the descaling interval.

## 5. Priming the Hydraulic Circuit

Fill the reservoir to the MAX line and press the power button. On first use, the display shows PRIME. Place a container under the hot water spout and press OK. The pump circulates water through the hydraulic circuit and the thermoblock heater for about 40 seconds. Priming must be repeated whenever the reservoir runs completely dry, otherwise air in the circuit causes low pump pressure.

## 6. Initial Grinder Setting

The conical burr grinder has 9 settings: 1 is the finest and 9 is the coarsest. The factory setting is 5. Adjust the grind setting only while the grinder is running, using the grind adjustment key, and turn it by one step at a time.
